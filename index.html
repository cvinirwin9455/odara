How Odara Works

A structured, AI-assisted workflow that strengthens every coaching engagement.

Upload & Discover

Clients upload CVs, portfolios, work samples, and artefacts. AI identifies skills, estimates levels, and shows the evidence behind each assessment.

Explore & Plan

AI-guided conversations explore career directions. Odara maps skills to roles, identifies gaps, and suggests realistic transition paths — not just dream destinations.

Develop & Progress

Personalised development paths go beyond courses. Track progress, upload new evidence, gather feedback, and see readiness estimates for target roles.
